This lesson practices one move:

> Do not begin with “I have so much material.” Begin with the concrete outcome you want to produce.

[[en/ai-collaboration-learning-lab|← Back to “Turn Ideas into Outcomes with AI”]]

## The Core Idea

AI is good at expanding, organizing, comparing, and drafting.

But if you only give it a pile of input, it may organize everything thoroughly without producing what you actually need.

Instead of saying:

```text
Help me organize this material.
```

Start with something like:

```text
I want to turn this material into a brief I can use to make a decision.
```

Or:

```text
I want to turn this material into a 70% draft that I will revise afterward.
```

## The Output-First Prompt

Begin with three sentences:

1. **Input**: What I just saw or experienced was…
2. **Question**: What I actually need to learn or decide is…
3. **Output**: Please first create a…, which I will revise with my own judgment.

For example:

```text
What I just read: several articles about saving tokens and managing context in AI agents.
What I actually need to decide: whether these techniques are worth integrating into my current AI workflow.
Please first create a decision note covering useful techniques, suitable situations, risks, and the next test.
```

## Why This Works Better

AI needs to know what kind of “done” you want.

The same input can lead to very different results depending on the output:

| Desired output | What AI will tend to do                  |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Summary        | Compress the material                    |
| Decision note  | Compare options and tradeoffs            |
| Article draft  | Find a central idea, structure, and tone |
| Checklist      | Extract a repeatable process             |
| Project action | Turn the material into executable work   |
| Reference note | Build reusable knowledge                 |

Output-first is not only a format. It sets the direction of the collaboration.

## Use It Next Time

Before giving AI a new input, pause for ten seconds and choose an output type.

This shifts AI from being an organizer to becoming a collaborator on a concrete outcome.

If you do not know what the output should be, ask:

```text
What are the three most useful concrete outcomes this input could become? Rank them by their value to my current goal.
```
