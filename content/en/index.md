This is where I publicly work through ideas, lived experience, and experiments.

I'm Levi, a software engineer based in Berlin. I use AI, engineering, writing, and product judgment to turn vague ideas and requirements into outcomes people can understand, use, and verify. This site records the judgment that takes shape along the way, not just the final answers.

The writing continues to evolve as I gain new experience. To see the websites, products, and engineering work I've built, visit [JL Studio / Levi Portfolio](https://jlstudio.xyz/levi).

### Form Judgment with AI

- [[en/let-ai-grill-you|Don't Just Ask AI—Let AI Ask You Questions Too]] — Use a multi-turn conversation to make vague but important work clearer.

- [[en/ai-era-role-positioning|In the AI Era, the Role That Fits Me Is Not a Job Title]] — How different forms of work can build the same capabilities without losing a current focus.

### Turn Ideas into Outcomes and Systems

If the direction is becoming clear, the next step is to bring your understanding into contact with reality and develop ways of working you can keep using:

- [[en/organizing-ai-tasks|When AI Starts Handling Different Kinds of Work, How Should You Organize Them?]] — Use Main, ongoing, and one-off Tasks to give different kinds of work clear ownership and context.
- [[en/input-to-concrete-output|From Input to Concrete Output Is a Skill You Can Train]] — Move from collecting, understanding, and ideating toward outcomes that can be checked, used, or shared.
