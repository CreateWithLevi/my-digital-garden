Concrete Outcome Impact Map

Move from “I made something” toward an outcome that can be used, receive feedback, and—when appropriate—develop into a product or service.

The Core Path

1. Output
Article, lesson, template, demo, or case study.
2. Destination
A current project, Digital Garden, repository, portfolio, or conversation.
3. Use
Let one real person or situation understand, test, or respond to it.
4. Feedback
Use results, questions, counterexamples, comments, direct messages, or new needs.
5. Offer
Template, workshop, service, consulting, or product.
6. Business
Client work, career opportunity, paid content, digital product, or SaaS.

Destination Guide

Destination Best suited for Main question
Current project or work Decisions, specs, processes, prototypes Does it directly improve the next action?
Digital Garden Long-form writing, public notes, lessons, case studies Can another person understand it and bring it into their own context?
GitHub Repositories, skills, prompts, HTML labs, demos Can the outcome be inspected, used, or forked?
Portfolio or service page Case studies, service processes, selected outcomes Does it demonstrate capability and judgment?
Community or conversation Self-contained ideas, questions, short cases Are there genuinely relevant people here?

Minimum Experiment

Begin with: Choose one completed concrete outcome → find one person or situation that genuinely needs it → let them use or respond to it → update the original outcome from one specific piece of feedback.
