Practice: Turn an Input into a Concrete Outcome

This section contains only the interactive exercise. See the surrounding lesson for the full idea, further reading, and course links.

Win condition: Turn one input into the next smallest concrete outcome.

Exercise 1: Choose the Outcome Type

The point is not to find the one correct answer. Practice judging which output will make the next step clearer.

Scenario A: You read an article about personal branding that relates to your own positioning, but you do not have a complete argument yet.

Scenario B: You notice that a product may ask users for sensitive information too early and want to clarify a better entry strategy.

Scenario C: You recognize the terms in a technical concept but cannot yet explain it clearly in your own words.
